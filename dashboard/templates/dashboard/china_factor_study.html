What we compare

We start with our list of {{ study.candidate_count }} companies. For each month, we check which shares have enough prices and financial data. We then compare three ways to invest in the same eligible group.

1. Equal stock weights

We give the same weight to each eligible company. This is our starting point.

2. Equal factor weights

We choose stocks using three factors. Each factor gets one-third of the portfolio.

3. Model factor weights

We predict next month's factor returns. The highest forecast gets 50%, then 30% and 20%.

How we run the study

We calculate Value, Profitability and Momentum for each eligible company. For each factor, we select the highest-ranked quarter of the group and use equal weights inside that basket.

Value is EBIT divided by our enterprise value estimate. We use the A-share price and total ordinary shares to estimate company value, then add debt and minority equity and subtract cash. Profitability is EBIT divided by average invested capital. We calculate invested capital as equity plus interest-bearing debt minus cash. Momentum uses the return from 12 months ago to one month ago, divided by volatility.

Financial companies stay in the wider stock benchmark. We leave them out of the factor baskets because their debt and capital have a different role.

We keep time in order. Models learn from earlier months and forecast a later month. We use an earlier validation period to choose a model, then compare results in a separate test period.

We use annual financial information after its reported release date and at least six months after the year-end. We rebalance after the monthly signal date. Saved financial statements may contain later revisions.

We chose this company list for our research and applied it to past data. The results describe this group and test period. The notebook contains the data checks, factor definitions and model settings.
