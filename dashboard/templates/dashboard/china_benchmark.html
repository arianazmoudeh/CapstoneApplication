How we build the basket

We check the {{ benchmark.candidate_count }} A-share codes in our list and keep companies listed by 31 December 2015 with enough price history for our 2016 start. {{ benchmark.stock_count }} companies are included and {{ benchmark.excluded_count }} are left out.

Each company starts with the same weight. At each month-end close, we reset the weights to {{ benchmark.target_weight|floatformat:2 }}% for the following trading session. Between those dates, the weights move as share prices change.

This basket gives us a reference with a fixed group from the start of 2016. Our factor study compares stock selection and model allocation using the 100-company list and monthly data checks.
